Prof. Dr. Ebtessam Nada

Excellence in Audio-Vestibular Medicine

Academic Journey

Prof. Dr. Ebtessam Nada is a Professor of Audio-Vestibular Medicine at the Otolaryngology Department, Zagazig University, Egypt. With extensive clinical and academic experience, she is dedicated to improving the standards of audiology and vestibular practice.

She is the founder and director of Audiology Step by Step, one of the leading educational platforms in the field.

Publications & Academic Work

Author of the educational book series “Audiology Step by Step”, consisting of six volumes covering clinical audiology, vestibular assessment, and more.

Editor for the Egyptian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology and reviewer for several international journals.

Beyond Audiology

Holding diplomas in Childhood Psychiatry and Therapeutic Nutrition, she believes in a holistic view of patient health — recognizing the interplay between neurological, psychological, and sensory systems.
